08 / Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge a callout fee in Paarl?

We provide free, no-obligation estimates for planned standard jobs and renovation consultations. For after-hours emergency callouts or comprehensive technical leak detections requiring specialized diagnostic equipment, a transparent callout fee applies, which we always confirm upfront before dispatching our vehicle.

How quickly can you arrive for emergencies?

Because our technicians are based directly in Paarl, our average arrival time across Paarl, Wellington, and Franschhoek is between 30 and 45 minutes for urgent situations like burst geysers, overflowing sewers, and flooded main lines.

Do you provide PIRB Certificates of Compliance (CoC)?

Yes. All our geyser replacements and substantial plumbing installations comply with SANS 10252 and SANS 10254 standards, and we issue official PIRB Certificates of Compliance required for homeowner insurance claims and property transfers.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT), instant EFT, cash, and debit/credit cards. For large bathroom renovations or commercial projects, structured milestone payments can be arranged.

Do you operate over weekends and public holidays?

Yes. We operate standard working hours Monday through Saturday, and our emergency on-call team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Sundays and public holidays.

Can you assist with insurance claims for burst geysers?

Absolutely. We regularly work with major South African insurance providers. We provide comprehensive photo evidence, incident reports, and itemized tax invoices to ensure your insurance claim is processed smoothly.
